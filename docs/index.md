Beckett is the non-interactive OODA heartbeat runner for
[Pirandello](https://github.com/ghelleks/pirandello)-style Role workspaces.
It reads your `OODA.md` agenda, runs pre-flight guard scripts, and
conditionally invokes Claude — on a cron schedule, with no human in the loop.

The posts below walk through each component from the end-user's point of view:
what problem it solves, how it works, and a copy-pasteable walkthrough to get
it running.
